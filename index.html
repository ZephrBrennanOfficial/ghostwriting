The Problem

LinkedIn matters. Nobody has time for it.

Every founder knows LinkedIn drives inbound leads, talent pipelines, and industry credibility. The data backs it up. But writing falls to the bottom of the priority list.

The reality

Running a company, managing teams, closing deals — that comes first. By the time there's a free hour, staring at a blank LinkedIn text box is the last thing anyone wants to do.

The result: a dormant profile. The last post was months ago. Meanwhile, competitors post consistently and capture the attention of prospects, partners, and talent.

The solution

Quill writes the posts. Not generic thought leadership. Content in the client's voice — their opinions, their stories, their industry perspective.

15 minutes of review per week. The writing, research, and editing are handled. The LinkedIn presence becomes what it should be — an active, authoritative asset.

The Service

What Quill delivers

No vague "personal brand building." Here's the actual work.

1

Voice capture

A 45-minute onboarding call to learn how the client speaks — phrasing, opinions, humour, tone. Every post is written in that voice, not a generic corporate one.

2

Post writing

15 posts per month. Each post opens with a scroll-stopping hook, delivers something worth reading, and ends with a point that drives conversation. 150–300 words. Concise, sharp, human.

3

Review and approval

Every post is delivered in a shared document. The client reviews, requests changes, or approves. If it doesn't sound right, it gets revised. The client always has final say.

4

Performance tracking

Engagement data is monitored — which posts land, which don't. The content strategy adapts based on what's working. Posts get sharper every month.

5

Topic strategy

Help identifying what to post about — industry takes, founder journey, hiring philosophy, contrarian opinions. The ideas that are easy to talk about but hard to write down.

How It Works

The process

1

Onboarding call — 45 min

A structured Google Meet session. Questions are asked, the client talks, notes are taken. The goal is to capture voice — phrasing, opinions, humour, frustration points. This call is the foundation for every post that follows.

2

First 4 posts — free

Four posts are drafted in the client's voice and delivered in a shared document. If they don't sound right, they're revised. If they do, the client posts the first one. No charge. If the fit isn't right, walk away with no obligation.

3

Publish, track, refine

The client publishes on their schedule. Engagement is tracked — likes, comments, shares. Topics that resonate are identified and doubled down on. Each month's content is informed by the previous month's data.

4

Ongoing — 15 posts/month

Every month: 15 posts written, delivered, and ready for review. 15 minutes of the client's time per week. The LinkedIn presence stays active, authentic, and consistent — without the time cost.

FAQ

Common questions

How does the voice capture work? ⌄
A 45-minute onboarding call is conducted to learn how the client speaks — phrasing, opinions, tone, humour. Notes are taken and used as the foundation for every post. The client reviews each post and can request revisions until it sounds right.
What if the posts don't sound right? ⌄
The first 4 posts are free. If they don't fit the client's voice, they're revised. If the fit still isn't right, the client walks away — no cost, no obligation. There's zero risk to trying.
Does the client post the content themselves? ⌄
Yes. The client reviews, approves, and publishes. This keeps the account authentic and the client in control. Scheduling assistance via Buffer (free) is available for batch posting.
How long are the posts? ⌄
150–300 words. Long enough to say something meaningful, short enough to read in 30 seconds. No padding, no long intros, no filler.
Can the client edit posts before publishing? ⌄
Absolutely. The posts belong to the client. Tweak, edit, rewrite — whatever's needed.
Is there a lock-in contract? ⌄
No. Cancel anytime. No exit fees, no awkward conversations.
Is AI used to write the posts? ⌄
AI tools are used for research and brainstorming support. Every post is written by a human, in the client's voice, based on the onboarding conversation. No AI-generated "thought leadership." The writing is human. Always.
